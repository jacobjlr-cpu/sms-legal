Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 11, 2026

This Privacy Policy covers the text-message line operated by Jacob Robinson (sole proprietor), which sends messages under the demo business name "Jacob's Demo Snow & Lawn". It explains what information we collect when you call or text this line and how we use it.

Information we collect

  • Your phone number, when you call or text the line.
  • The messages and photos you send us, and any voicemail you leave (including a written transcript of it).
  • Details you choose to share in the conversation, such as your name, address, and what you need help with.
  • Basic records of each call and message (date, time, and delivery status).

How we use it

  • To reply to you by text about your call or message.
  • To pass a short summary of your request to the business owner so they can follow up with you.
  • To keep the service working reliably and to stop spam or abuse.

Replies on this line may be written by an automated assistant. It only collects your details and passes them on; it does not make sales offers.

Sharing

We do not sell or share your SMS opt-in data or personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

We use service providers only to run the line: Twilio (to send and receive calls and texts) and Anthropic (to process message text so the automated assistant can reply). They handle your information only to provide these services to us.

How long we keep it

Conversations, photos and voicemails are deleted after 90 days.

Your choices

Reply STOP at any time to stop all texts from this line. Reply START to resume. To ask about or delete your information, email jacobjlr@umich.edu.
